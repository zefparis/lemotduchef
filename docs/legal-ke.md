## 2. The service

Le Mot du Chef helps restaurant owners reply to their Google reviews. You add a review (by pasting it or by forwarding Google's review notification email to your personal address), and the service suggests three replies, in the language of the review, using artificial intelligence. The service also offers printable materials with a QR code that leads to your Google review page.

The replies are suggestions. Nothing is ever published without your action: you read, edit if needed, and publish each reply yourself.

# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how IA Solution collects and uses personal data when you use Le Mot du Chef through ke.lemotduchef.fr. It is written to meet the requirements of the Kenyan Data Protection Act, 2019, and of the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is IA Solution (details in our Legal notice).
Contact for data protection matters: Benjamin Barrère, contact@ia-solution.fr.

## 2. Data we collect

- **Account**: your name, email address and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form), your language and preferences.
- **Restaurant profile**: the name of your restaurant, the information you choose to give about it (tone, strengths, contact details for customers), your signature.
- **Reviews and replies**: the reviews you paste or forward to us, the replies we suggest and the replies you mark as published. Reviews may contain the first name of the person who wrote them, as published on Google.
- **Forwarded emails**: if you set up forwarding, the Google review notification emails sent to your personal address.
- **Billing**: your subscription status and payment history. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by us.
- **Notifications**: if you enable them, a technical identifier for each device that receives notifications.
- **Technical data**: connection logs and security information (IP address, browser, session dates).

## 3. Why we use it

- to provide the service and your account (performance of our contract with you);
- to process payments and keep accounting records (contract and legal obligations);
- to send you service messages such as account verification, password reset and billing information (contract);
- to secure the service and prevent abuse (our legitimate interest);
- to improve the service using aggregated statistics (our legitimate interest).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your personal data for commercial direct marketing without your consent.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

To suggest replies, the text of the review and the relevant information about your restaurant are sent to our AI provider. Before sending, we replace the first name of the review's author with a neutral marker, and we put it back only in the reply shown to you. Our AI provider processes this data on our behalf through its commercial service, does not use it to train its models, and keeps it only for a short period for security purposes.

## 5. Service providers

We share data only with providers who help us run the service, under contracts that require them to protect it:

- Anthropic PBC (United States): AI model used to suggest replies;
- Stripe (United States and Ireland): payment processing;
- Resend (United States): sending service emails;
- Vercel Inc. (United States): hosting of the website front end;
- OVHcloud (France): email services, including the mailbox that receives forwarded reviews;
- Cloudflare (United States): network security and routing.

Our application and database run on servers operated by IA Solution in France.

## 6. Transfers outside Kenya

Your data is stored in France, within the European Union, and some providers listed above are located in the United States. We transfer data outside Kenya only with appropriate safeguards for its security and protection, such as binding contracts including standard contractual clauses, as required by the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the GDPR.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Account and restaurant profile: as long as your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closure, except where we must keep some data by law.
- Reviews and suggested replies: 12 months after their creation, then the review text is deleted.
- Billing records: as long as required by accounting and tax law (up to 10 years under French law).
- Technical logs: up to 12 months.

## 8. Your rights

You may at any time ask to be informed about the use of your data, to access it, to correct or delete it, to object to its processing, or to receive a copy of it in a portable format. You can export your data and close your account from "My account", or write to contact@ia-solution.fr. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (odpc.go.ke) or, in France, with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities without undue delay if a breach affects you.

## 10. Cookies

The website uses only the cookies and local storage strictly needed to run the service (for example to keep you signed in and remember your language and display preferences). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
